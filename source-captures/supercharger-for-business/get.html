Supercharger Pricing Details

V4 Cabinet, V4 Posts

Tesla's next generation V4 Supercharger Post has a max power of 500 kW and is designed to deliver best-in-class performance, user experience, and aesthetics. The V4 Supercharger Post brings Tesla's global leadership in EV charging infrastructure deployment to more use cases by enabling support for global charging standards, including NACS, CCS1, CCS2, and GB/T. The V4 Supercharger Post with universal cable reach delivers a vehicle-agnostic experience allowing all users to charge with confidence.

The Tesla V4 Cabinet provides AC/DC power conversion as part of the Supercharger system when deployed with the V4 Supercharger Post. The advanced system architecture includes highly optimized state of the art power electronics, efficient conversion, and enables powersharing for up to 8 Supercharger Posts.

Other Hardware

Each Supercharger system includes hardware to support the post and cabinet. Specifically, DC cable, credit card reader (if required), site controller (where needed), and connectivity infrastructure.

Services

To help ensure your project's success, Tesla provides project engineering and commissioning services. Your assigned Tesla project engineer will:

  • Answer design-related questions
  • Review design drawings before permitting submissions
  • Review pre-commissioning deliverables after construction
  • Schedule the commissioning visit
  • Provide commissioning support (managed by Tesla Service technicians)

Shipping

Shipping and logistics to your installation address.
